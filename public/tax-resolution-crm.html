What is a tax resolution CRM?

A tax resolution CRM is customer and case-management software designed for firms that help taxpayers resolve IRS and state tax matters. Unlike a generic CRM, the system needs to support case-oriented workflows, client documents, deadlines, communications, staff collaboration, and tax-resolution operations in one place.
